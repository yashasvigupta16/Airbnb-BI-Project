# 🌎 Global Airbnb Trends & Performance Dashboard
A Power BI project analysing global Airbnb listings and guest reviews to uncover market trends, host performance, and customer behaviour across global cities.

![alt text](image/MainPage.png)

## 📌 Project Overview 
Discover key insights from Airbnb listings, ratings, and guest reviews to understand market trends and customer behaviour across global cities. This report covers **2,79,712 listings** across **10 cities**, **1,82,024 hosts**, **144 property types**, and **5.37M reviews**

- **Data Source :** Maven Analytics
- **Tools Used :** Power BI, Power Query, DAX
- **Created By :** Yashasvi Gupta

## 📊 Dashboard Pages 
### 1. Main Page
Landing page introducing the report, with navigation cards into Overview, Ratings, and Reviews, plus report metadata (last refresh data, data source, tools used)
### 2. Overview
A high-level snapshot of Airbnb's presence :

- **2,79,712** total listings acroos **10** cities
- **1,82,024** hosts and **144** property types
- **5,373K** total reviews
- **New Listings Growth Trend (2008-2020) :** Tracks Airbnb's lifecycle through Introduction, Growth, Maturity, Decline, Reinvention, and the COVID-19 impact — broken out by Entire Place, Hotel Room, Private Rooms, and Shared Room. Listings took off in 2011, peaked in 2015, began declining in 2016, slowed in 2017, showed signs of recovery from 2018, then dropped sharply after COVID-19 hit the travel industry.
### 3.Ratings
A deep dive into market share, pricing, and guest satisfaction:

- **Market Share By City:** Paris,New York, and Sydney account for nearly half(48%) of all Airbnb listings and reviews, shown via a stacked chart with superhost vs. non superhost listings and cumulative % . Paris leads both listings and reviews, driven by consistent year-round tourism.
- **Average Price By Room Type :** Hotel Room($800), Entire place($673), Shared Room($580),Private Room ($462)
- **Detailed Rtaings By City :** Scores across Accuracy,Cleanliness, Location and Value for all cities. Mexico City and Rio De Janerio rank highest overall, while Hong Kong and Istanbul rank lowest.
Communication scores highest on average across cities; Cleanliness and Value for Money score lowest.

### 4. Reviews
An analysis of review activity, seasonality, and host trustworthiness:

- **Review Frequency :** 86.5% of customers wrote only one review, and 98.8% wrote three or fewer — showing repeat reviewers are rare. One outlier reviewer left 283 reviews, with the last two posted in Bangkok on 13 october 2020.
- **Seasonality :** Monthly review volume by city (Mexioco City, New York, Pris, Rome,Sydney). Paris and Rome maintain consistently high review volume year-round; New York sees noticeable spike in November-December, likely tied to holiday travel.
- **Trust Factor :** 66.9% of hosts are fully verified(idenetity verified + profile picture). Only 0.3% of hosts lack both trust signals, keeping unverified/anonymous profiles to a minimum.

## 🎯 Key Insights
- Paris, New York, and Sydney together drive nearly half of gloabal listings and reviews
- Airbnb's growth peaked in 2015 before declining, recovering briefly pre-COVID, then dropping sharply in 2020
- Mexico City and Rio De Janerio are top-rated cities; Communication is guest's highest-rated experience factor
- The vast majority of guests are one-time reviewers, and most hosts(66.9%)
carry both trust signals(verification + profile photo)
